OUR STORY

About Mulzon.

A growing ecosystem of businesses and technology built with purpose, craftsmanship and a practical outlook from Ahmedabad, India.

WHO WE ARE

A home for ideas that make a practical difference.

Mulzon is an umbrella organization bringing together business ventures and technology products under one roof. Our portfolio spans architectural glass and mirror, premium hardware, business management software and people operations tools.

Each venture has its own purpose and audience. What connects them is a shared commitment to useful solutions, considered experiences and the everyday work of building something that lasts.

Founded in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, we are proud to build India-quality products that serve businesses and spaces across the country.


Work With Us

Established in Ahmedabad

Our journey began with physical materials — glass and mirror. Over time, as we saw the need for better operations, we started building our own technology. Today, we do both.
