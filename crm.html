Turn leads into organized opportunities.

Every enquiry your business receives deserves to be tracked, assigned, and followed through. GlassEntials CRM gives each lead a place, an owner, and a history.

No more sticky notes, WhatsApp messages or scattered Excel sheets. Leads come in, get captured, and move through your process.

  • Assign leads to team members
